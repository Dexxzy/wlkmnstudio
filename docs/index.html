your walkman, but actually yours.

a little open-source app i built to theme and tune a rooted sony nw-a50 — boot animation, splash, fonts, any-color UI, plus audio and quality-of-life mods. started with my own player and got happily out of hand.

everything's backed up, md5-verified, and reversible. no sony files bundled — it only touches your device's own assets and whatever you upload.

see it on github

what it does

  • theme — boot animation, power-on splash, UI font, icon accent, and a full text themer (any color, per element).
  • audio — force LDAC 990, raise SBC bitpool, the A2DP/AirPods fix, and a live bluetooth codec monitor.
  • quality of life — kill the boot-time database rebuild for way faster startups, plus storage tools and one-click backups.
needs walkman one on a rooted nw-a50. built to sit next to mr walkman's firmware and wampy, not replace them. not affiliated with sony — just a fan.
